log-diagonal Cholesky parameterization instrument

DefinitionWrite a symmetric positive definite matrix as L times its transpose, with L lower triangular, and store the diagonal of L as logarithms. The n(n+1)/2 stored numbers are unconstrained, and any real values give a symmetric positive definite matrix.
ExampleFor a 2 by 2 matrix, log-diagonal entries of 0 and 0 with an off-diagonal entry of 1 give L with rows (1, 0) and (1, 1), and the product has rows (1, 1) and (1, 2), with determinant 1. A 9 by 9 matrix needs 36 + 9 = 45 numbers.

Assumptions and scope
  • The map from the parameters to the positive definite matrices is smooth, onto, and has a smooth inverse, the Cholesky decomposition followed by the logarithm of the diagonal.
  • Setting every parameter to zero gives the identity matrix, the volume’s starting point for optimization.
