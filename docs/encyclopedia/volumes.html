The Geometric Series has fourteen volumes. The entries of Volume 14, Geometric Observation, and of its textbook are built from the program's claim ledger and measurement records, and carry ledger classes. The entries of the other volumes are curated from each volume's text. Each cites the passage that states it, and carries the standing the volume gives its own claims in place of a ledger class.
