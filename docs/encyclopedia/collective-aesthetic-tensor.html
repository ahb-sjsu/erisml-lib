Collective aesthetic tensor concept

DefinitionA multilinear map that takes one interest covector from each reader in a population and returns a collective valence direction on the manifold. For two readers it splits into each reader's individual contribution plus an emergent component, the part of collective valence not attributable to any individual.
ExampleFor two readers and the chapter’s nine working axes the tensor has 9 times 9 times 9, which is 729 components. That is the volume’s count.
VolumeGeometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series; entry id collective-aesthetic-tensor, kind concept.
StatusPosited. No reader-level interest covectors are measured in the volume, and the Lasso axes are regression directions on aggregate ratings. Corrections: none recorded.
Defining equationnone
Assumptions and scope
  • The emergent component vanishes exactly when collective valence is the sum of the individual valences.
  • Appendix F marks canon formation as geometric aggregation of individual judgments as conjectured, with no direct empirical fit (F14.1).
  • The evidence offered for a nonzero emergent component is the 71 Lasso axes. The chapter’s own caveat is that most of those axes do genre discrimination and that the point is structural, not a matter of effect size.
  • The count of 729 components assumes nine working axes per reader. Section 5.5 says the aesthetic manifold, unlike the moral one, does not start from nine known dimensions.
