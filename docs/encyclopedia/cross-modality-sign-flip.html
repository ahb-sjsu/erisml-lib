Cross-modality sign flip result

DefinitionTwo channels have opposite signs of association with reception in books and in music. Internal coherence is positive for book rating ($\rho = +0.126$) and negative for music listens ($\rho = -0.076$). Mean step size is negative for books ($\rho = -0.096$) and positive for music ($\rho = +0.071$).
ExampleThe volume’s table gives pair_sim_mean a correlation of +0.126 with book rating and -0.076 with music listens. The two have opposite signs, and so do -0.096 and +0.071 for step_mean.
VolumeGeometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series; entry id cross-modality-sign-flip, kind result.
StatusVerified empirically in Appendix F (F21.6, F21.7). The reading that books reward continuity and music rewards contrast is the volume's interpretation, and Chapter 12 treats the flip as a broken symmetry of its invariance claim. Corrections: none recorded.

Assumptions and scope
  • Two modalities only. The targets also differ (Goodreads rating for books, log listens for music), and so do the encoders (LaBSE and MERT).
  • The volume has not ruled out that the flip comes from the encoders. Section 17.9 and Appendix E.4 keep that as a legitimate alternative reading.
  • Appendix E says the flip was checked after the Hellinger fix and persists. Section 12.5 says genre residualization preserves both flips.
  • Extension of the pattern to film, visual art or architecture is marked conjectured in Appendix F (F22.1, F29.4). The README lists cross-modal sign flips among the reasons the AesBSE instrument was ruled inadmissible.
