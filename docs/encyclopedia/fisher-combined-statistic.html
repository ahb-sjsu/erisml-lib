Fisher-combined significance instrument

DefinitionThe statistic $-2 \sum_i \ln p_i$ over $k$ independent p-values, one per model. Under the null it follows a chi-squared distribution with $2k$ degrees of freedom. The volume converts the combined p-value to a sigma value with the standard normal quantile function and reports its headline results in this form.
ExampleTwo p-values of 0.01 give -2 times (ln 0.01 + ln 0.01) = 18.42, to be compared with a chi-squared distribution with 4 degrees of freedom.
VolumeGeometric Reasoning, Volume 3 of the Geometric Series; entry id fisher-combined-statistic, kind instrument.
StatusA standard statistical method. The five headline sigma values it produces are measured benchmark results whose independence assumption the volume itself says is imperfect. Corrections: none recorded.
Defining equationnone
Assumptions and scope
  • The method is tagged established mathematics and assumes independent tests. The volume grants that the five models share training methods and that four share a lineage.
  • The volume calls the combined test anti-conservative under positive dependence, so the true significance is lower than reported.
  • Each cell holds 5 to 25 evaluations, so the per-model p-values that enter the sum may be imprecise.
  • A combined sigma value is a statement about the five tested models taken together. The volume limits it to transformer language models from two laboratories.
