Political Bond Invariance Principle concept

DefinitionA democratic evaluation, such as an election, a poll or a representation assessment, is epistemically well-posed if and only if it is invariant under voter anonymity, option neutrality and re-description invariance.
ExampleSuppose the same voters with the same preferences elect A when A is listed first and B when B is listed first. The outcome depends on the labeling of the ballot, so the election fails option neutrality and is not well-posed under the principle.
