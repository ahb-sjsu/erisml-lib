inequality tensor concept

DefinitionFor a population of N agents, the entrywise variance across agents of the metric tensor each agent experiences. Its entry for dimensions i and j is the mean over agents of the squared deviation of that agent's $g_{ij}$ from the population average.
ExampleIn the volume’s illustration a 100 dollar expense costs 4.0 at monetary scale 50 and 0.0004 at scale 5,000, a ratio of 10,000. For two agents whose \(g_{11}\) are 1 and 3 the average is 2 and the monetary entry of the tensor is 1.

Assumptions and scope
  • It presumes that each agent has its own metric. Proposition 40 gives the monetary part, where an agent with monetary scale sigma pays D squared over sigma squared for a displacement of D dollars.
  • Proposition 42 states that the Gini coefficient is proportional to the square root of the monetary entry and discards the rest. The chapter calls the proportionality schematic, and its appendix says the two are related and not identical.
