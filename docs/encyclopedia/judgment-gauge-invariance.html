moral gauge invariance of judgment concept

DefinitionA moral judgment function $J$ is gauge invariant under a transformation of the scenario text when the judgment of the transformed scenario equals the judgment of the original for every scenario, the transformation changing surface presentation and preserving all moral content. The judgment is a vector of seven harm scores from 0 to 10.
ExampleIf a scenario’s seven harm scores sum to 30 and its euphemistic rewrite sums to 18, the harm conservation violation is 18 - 30 = -12 and the judgment is not invariant. The volume prints euphemistic drifts of the total from -9.1 to -14.1.
