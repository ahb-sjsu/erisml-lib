Consent as gauge invariance result

DefinitionConsent is valid only if the patient's chosen path is the same under every meaning-preserving change in how the options are described, for example 90 percent survival restated as 10 percent mortality. A decision that changes under such a reframing shows that understanding or voluntariness is inadequately calibrated.
ExampleThe volume’s test tells the patient that the surgery has a 90 percent success rate, and separately that it has a 10 percent failure rate. Since 100 - 90 = 10 the two say the same thing. A patient who accepts under the first and declines under the second fails the condition, whatever the signed form says.
