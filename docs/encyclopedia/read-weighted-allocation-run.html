read-weighted allocation, the recorded run of Section 22.4 result

DefinitionOn a synthetic consumer that reads 3 directions of an 8-dimensional Gaussian source, reverse water-filling against a read metric estimated from 200 probe points gave 0.476, 0.334 and 0.196 of the consumer loss of the identity-weighted allocation at 1, 2 and 4 nats. Its reconstruction error was larger at every rate.
ExampleAt 4 nats the volume’s table gives consumer losses of 0.033 and 0.170 and reconstruction errors of 0.722 and 0.307. The loss ratio from these rounded values is 0.19, where the record gives 0.196, and the reconstruction ratio is 2.4.
