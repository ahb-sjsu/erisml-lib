No Escape Theorem for Educational AI result

DefinitionAn AI system working on the learner manifold is bound by its structure in four ways. It cannot recommend skipping a prerequisite, cannot assess dimensions it does not measure, cannot optimize a scalar objective without the divergence of Theorem 5.3, and cannot give gauge-invariant assessment without evaluation across formats, contexts and modes.
ExampleAn autograder that measures 2 of the 6 learner dimensions leaves 6 minus 2, which is 4, dimensions that Corollary 5.2.1 says it cannot infer. The volume’s case is a grader that runs test cases and sees domain knowledge and procedural skill only.

Assumptions and scope
  • The text gives a proof sketch. Each part is referred to an earlier statement, namely the prerequisite connectivity of the manifold, Corollary 5.2.1, Theorem 5.3 and Theorem 9.1.
