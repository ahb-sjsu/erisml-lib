geometric taxonomy of cognitive pathology concept

DefinitionThe volume's classification of four conditions by the geometric object that is defective. ADHD is instability of the attention metric, autism is rigidity of the attention metric, depression is a collapsed heuristic field with near-zero gradient, and anxiety is an inflated heuristic field that overestimates cost.
ExampleThe volume’s inflation factor is the estimated cost divided by the true cost. A true cost of 2 estimated as 6 gives a factor of 3, far above the value near 1 the volume assigns to healthy cognition, which is its picture of anxiety.
