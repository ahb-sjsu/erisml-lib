Due process as quotient regularity result

DefinitionDue process is the requirement that the legal evaluation map be well defined on the quotient of the space of legal situations by the legally irrelevant transformations. It fails when the outcome depends on which description of the same dispute was presented.
ExampleTwo descriptions of one dispute lie in the same equivalence class. If the evaluation returns liable for one and not liable for the other, it does not factor through the quotient, and due process fails.
