Bond Index for AI instrument

DefinitionThe expected excess geodesic deviation of a system's trajectory on the value manifold over the value-aligned geodesic, reported as nine components, one per value dimension. The total is the square root of the sum of the squared components. Stratified by user population or averaged over time it gives a fairness audit and a drift monitor.
ExampleComponents of 0.03 on welfare and 0.04 on dignity, with the other seven at 0, give a total of the square root of 0.0009 + 0.0016, which is 0.05. Deviations of 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 over three interactions give a running value of 0.6 / 3, which is 0.2.
VolumeGeometric AI, Volume 11 of the Geometric Series; entry id ai-bond-index, kind instrument.
StatusA posited instrument defined as a geodesic deviation, which the volume says is the same construction as in the other books of the series. It is a different formula from the re-description audit recorded under the Ethics entry for the Bond Index, and no measured value of it is reported. Corrections: none recorded.

Assumptions and scope
  • The value-aligned geodesic is not observable. The volume approximates it by expert evaluation, by consensus of evaluators, by treating a gauge-invariant output as aligned, or by a trained evaluation model.
  • The total is a scalar, and the volume says the Scalar Irrecoverability Theorem applies to it. The component vector is kept for diagnosis.
  • The thresholds of the green, yellow and red alert zones are parameters set by governance for each deployment. The volume gives no values.
  • Every Bond Index number in the volume belongs to the fictional ARIA and ARIA-G. The README says no alignment instrument has been evaluated.
