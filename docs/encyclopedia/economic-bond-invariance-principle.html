economic Bond Invariance Principle and gauge group concept

DefinitionAn economic evaluation J satisfies the principle when J of a re-described state equals J of the state for every meaning-preserving transformation, one that changes labels, units or accounting conventions and not the economic reality. The transformations form the economic gauge group, scalings of the monetary dimension by a positive real together with permutations of the numeraire.
ExampleThe experiment of Shafir, Diamond and Tversky as the volume reports it. A 2 percent raise under 4 percent inflation is a real change of -2 percent and was rated better than a 1 percent cut under zero inflation, a real change of -1 percent. The rating followed the label, which violates the principle.
