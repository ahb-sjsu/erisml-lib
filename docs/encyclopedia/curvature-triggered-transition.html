curvature-triggered transition (System 1 to System 2) concept

DefinitionThe volume's geometric form of dual-process theory. A system follows the gradient of its heuristic field (System 1) where the curvature of the cognitive manifold is below a threshold, and computes geodesics (System 2) where the curvature reaches it.
ExampleIn the volume’s illustration a framing perturbation of 0.1 that produces a displacement of 0.3 is an amplification of 3.0, and over five reasoning steps \(e^{5s} = 3\) gives \(s = (ln 3)/5\), about 0.22, so the curvature magnitude is about 0.048. The inputs 0.1, 0.3 and 5 are modelled values.
