substitution distance instrument

DefinitionReplacing ingredient j by j' at amount $a_j$ moves the dish, to first order, by $\Delta\mathbf{f} \approx a_j(\mathbf{f}_{j'} - \mathbf{f}_j)$. The substitution is safe when this displacement is short in the flavor metric. Agreement on one coordinate, such as sourness, does not guarantee a short distance.
ExampleOn the axes (sour, citrus aroma) let lime be (4, 3), lemon (4, 2.5) and distilled vinegar (4.5, 0). At amount 2 with a Euclidean metric the lemon swap moves the dish by 2 times 0.5 = 1. The vinegar swap moves it by 2 times the length of (0.5, -3), about 6.1. The lemon and vinegar case is the volume’s and the vectors are illustrative.
