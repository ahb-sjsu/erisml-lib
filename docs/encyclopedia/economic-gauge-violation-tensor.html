economic gauge violation tensor and Bond Index instrument

DefinitionFor a heuristic h, transformations i and states j, the entry $V_{ij}$ is h of the transformed state minus h of the state. An invariant heuristic has every entry zero, and the Frobenius norm of V is the total violation. The Bond Index of Chapter 4 is the same difference in absolute value divided by h of the state.
ExampleA heuristic that scores a wage offer 100 in annual terms and 110 in monthly terms has an entry of 10 for that transformation and state, and a Bond Index of 10 / 100 = 0.10.
