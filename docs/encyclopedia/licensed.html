licensed, not licensed concept

DefinitionA claim is licensed when it is a theorem checked in Lean in appendix C, or a measurement sealed with its commit hash before the run that survived its registered null and bar, and a chapter may rest on it. A claim is not licensed when it is exploratory, a retrospective fit, a single seed, or posited, and a chapter may mention it and not lean on it. How to use this book, and chapter 8 section 8.10. Also not licensed.
